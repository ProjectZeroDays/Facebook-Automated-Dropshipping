# Compliance & tax (TN)

- Entity: file a TN LLC (Secretary of State), get an EIN.
- TN has NO general business license, but register for TN Business Tax via TNTAP.
- Sales tax: Meta is the marketplace facilitator and collects/remits on shipped orders.
- 1099-K: federal threshold back to $20,000 AND 200 txns (2026). Some states lower.
- Keep records 7 years. Run `python tax.py --export` monthly into pnl/.
- Bank: separate business account; hold a reserve for 45-day buyer claims.
